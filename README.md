OdontaLab Dental Design Website Redesign
1. Website Overview
OdontaLab Dental Design is a Shenzhen-based digital dentistry and dental laboratory service provider specialising in outsourced dental CAD/CAM design and digital dental workflows.

The company serves dental laboratories, production centres, dental technicians and dental practices requiring professional digital dental design support.

The redesigned homepage focuses on positioning OdontaLab as a specialised B2B digital dental design partner. It highlights technical expertise, production-ready design services, quality control, predictable turnaround times and personalised support for international laboratory partners.
2. Main Improvements
1. Clearer Service Presentation and Technical Specialisation
- Reorganised the presentation of digital dental design services, including crown and bridge design, implant restorations, dentures, surgical guides and orthodontic solutions.
- Improved the distinction between fixed restorations, removable appliances, implant-supported designs and additional laboratory services.
- Strengthened the visibility of the company's specialised CAD/CAM capabilities.
- Addresses the challenge of presenting a broad technical service portfolio in a clear and accessible way for professional buyers.
2. Stronger B2B Positioning and Value Communication
- Refined the homepage messaging to position OdontaLab as an outsourced digital dental design partner rather than a general dental service provider.
- Emphasised relevant business differentiators, including 12–24-hour standard turnaround, senior designer review and one-to-one customer communication.
- Strengthened the communication of production-ready deliverables and support for individual laboratory protocols.
- Addresses the need to communicate why dental laboratories should partner with OdontaLab.
3. Improved Homepage Structure and Enquiry Journey
- Reorganised homepage content to create a clearer progression through company capabilities, services and business value.
- Improved the presentation of important contact and enquiry information.
- Refined the content hierarchy to help international dental laboratories understand the company's offering before making an enquiry.
- Addresses the previous challenge of connecting technical service information with the company's primary commercial objective of acquiring laboratory partners.
3. Design Improvements
The redesigned homepage introduces a more structured, professional and technology-focused visual presentation.

Key improvements include:
- Visual presentation: A refined design direction reflecting the technical nature of digital dentistry and dental CAD/CAM services.
- Layout: Improved organisation of service categories, technical capabilities and company differentiators.
- Typography: Clearer content hierarchy and improved readability across technical information.
- Consistency: More cohesive presentation of services, business messaging and supporting information.
- User experience: A more logical browsing journey for dental laboratories evaluating outsourcing partners.
- B2B communication: Greater emphasis on technical expertise, turnaround, quality control and personalised service.
